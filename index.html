Reading the measures: monthly changes use seasonally adjusted series where BLS publishes them (series BLS does not seasonally adjust are marked); annual changes are not seasonally adjusted. Every value in this snapshot comes from BLS — a BLS-published percent change or computed directly from BLS-published index levels. Nothing is modeled or estimated. October 2025 was not published by BLS (lapse in appropriations); for November 2025 BLS published annual changes but no 1-month changes for most series, so those cells are shown as unavailable. Relative-importance weights are not shown in this snapshot.

Two views, one dataset: “By expenditure group” follows the BLS item taxonomy; “By BLS release narrative” follows the news-release layout (Food → Energy → All items less food & energy). Both views draw on the same BLS-published series — switching views never changes a number.
